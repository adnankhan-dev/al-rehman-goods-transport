How to read this report

"Expected" is rebuilt from every completed order, diesel entry, bill settlement, and payment on record. An account created with a manually entered opening balance will show that opening amount as a standing difference — that is not an error. Use Apply Expected only when you are confident the recorded activity is the complete truth for that account; the stored balance is then overwritten. Individual vehicles are not listed because owner payments are spread across vehicles without per-vehicle records — reconcile at the vehicle-owner level.
